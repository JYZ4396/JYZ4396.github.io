Housing and health disparities across four countries

Before joining ComDisp, I surveyed residents in Jacksonville’s Health Zone 1 about their housing conditions and respiratory health. ComDisp builds on earlier work in Jacksonville and brings together researchers in the United States, Vietnam, Türkiye, and Ecuador to study how housing conditions and climate hazards relate to health.

My work with the U.S. modeling team

My work so far has been on the agent-based modeling side of the project. At UF, I help coordinate data collection and document its progress. Every two weeks, I meet with the U.S. modeling team, and I’m also learning how agent-based models work and how to interpret their results. What interests me about this approach is that it could help us explore questions that are difficult to answer with survey data alone. For example, how might a change in housing policy affect households facing the same hazard?
