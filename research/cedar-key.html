Coastal infrastructure priorities in Cedar Key

Who should pay for coastal infrastructure in Cedar Key, Florida, and who should manage it? Our team studied how survey respondents’ environmental views related to the groups they nominated. Residents also ranked local places, giving us a second way to examine their priorities.

My role
Research assistant · DTSC Lab
Period
2026
Setting
Cedar Key, Florida

Following participants through the analysis

I validated survey records and documented which respondents each analysis included. A resident could complete the environmental attitudes questions and leave a ranking unfinished. Tracing these differences helped us describe whose answers supported each result.

Writing and reviewing the methods

I drafted the manuscript’s methods section and reviewed model specifications and outputs with the team. I explained how item response models summarized environmental attitudes, how latent class analysis identified response patterns, and how the team analyzed stakeholder nominations and place rankings.
