Coastal infrastructure priorities in Cedar Key

Our team studied whether Cedar Key residents’ environmental views were related to their priorities for coastal infrastructure. I helped check how the team’s models were implemented and worked with the team to interpret the results. As part of that review, I went back to the survey to check which questions had missing responses.

Writing the methods section

When I drafted the manuscript’s methods section, one distinction I wanted to make clear was how residents expressed their preferences in two parts of the survey. For each question about funding, management, or whose needs should come first, residents nominated three stakeholder groups without ranking them. I explained why the multinomial model treated those three nominations as a set. In the next part of the survey, residents ranked 13 types of local places by importance. Here, the order mattered, so the team used a Plackett–Luce model to estimate which places residents tended to rank highest. Finally, I described how the team checked those estimates against each place’s average rank in the survey.
