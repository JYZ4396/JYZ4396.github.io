Coastal infrastructure priorities in Cedar Key

Who should pay for coastal infrastructure in Cedar Key, Florida, and who should manage it? Our team studied how survey respondents’ environmental views related to the groups they nominated. Residents also ranked local places, giving us a second way to examine their priorities.

My role
Research assistant · DTSC Lab
Period
2026
Setting
Cedar Key, Florida

Checking the models and survey responses

I helped check how the team’s models were implemented. As part of that review, I checked the survey items used in each model for missing responses. For example, a resident could answer the questions about environmental attitudes but leave the ranking of local places unfinished.

Interpreting the results and writing the methods

I worked with the team to interpret the results on residents’ environmental attitudes and rankings of local places. I also drafted the manuscript’s methods section. I explained how item response models summarized environmental attitudes, how latent class analysis identified response patterns, and how the team analyzed stakeholder nominations and place rankings.
