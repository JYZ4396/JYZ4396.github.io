Learning and assessment in disaster recovery training

GC-CREATE is developing training for people making decisions about disaster recovery and resilient construction. I reviewed five learner profiles and examined whether their responses fit the roles and scenarios the team had designed.

My role

Period

Questions for the training design

I reviewed research on when to give feedback, how to assess whether learners can transfer a skill to an unfamiliar scenario, and how to display activity completion alongside evidence of competency.
