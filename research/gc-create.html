Learning and assessment in disaster recovery training

GC-CREATE is developing training for people making decisions about disaster recovery and resilient construction. I reviewed five different learner personas (profiles) and examined whether each persona’s responses fit its assigned role in the scenarios the team had designed.

My role
GC-CREATE gamification review team · DTSC Lab
Period
2026
Setting
Disaster recovery education

Reviewing the scenarios

After each biweekly meeting, the core team shares its progress and the latest designs for the five learner personas. We then review the disaster scenarios for each persona and ask whether someone in that role would realistically face that situation and be responsible for the decisions described. We also tried developing an AI agent that could take on each persona’s role in a scenario so we could examine the decisions it would make.
