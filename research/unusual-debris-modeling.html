Predicting unusual debris in coastal cleanups

In a Python modeling course, I used a Markov chain to predict changes in the dominant category of unusual debris. Its limited predictive performance led me to try a related question at the level of an individual cleanup.

My role
Python course project and independent follow-up
Period
Earlier modeling work
Setting
Florida coast

What did the classifier learn?

I grouped cleanup events by the share of unusual items and trained classifiers to identify events in the top quarter. Gradient boosting performed best among the models I compared, but its performance dropped when I removed reporting year. Changes in reporting categories over time made it difficult to tell how much of the predictive signal came from changes in the debris itself.
