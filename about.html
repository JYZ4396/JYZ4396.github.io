About

I’m Jiayi Zhu, an undergraduate studying data science and economics at the University of Florida.

Research interests

My interest in data quality grew out of fieldwork. A resident can answer questions that never appear correctly on a dashboard, just as a county can experience a hurricane and have no volunteer cleanup report. I want to understand who is represented in a dataset and how those gaps affect the conclusions we draw from it.

Research experience

Disasters, Trust & Social Change Lab

I joined the lab as a research assistant in 2023 and became lab manager in August 2026. I coordinate weekly meetings, track project deadlines, and support new lab members preparing for fieldwork. My research has taken me to Cedar Key, Keystone Heights, and Jacksonville.
